## Selection criteria for the morning digest

Skip:
- Hype posts without numbers or demos
- Pure marketing announcements with no technical substance
- Anything more than 48 hours old (we ran yesterday)
- Tooling tutorials and "10 prompts that…" listicles
- Generic "AI will change everything" takes

Prefer:
- New model releases with measurable claims
- Papers with a clean result and a method that's at least sketched
- Hands-on demos that show non-obvious behavior
- Industry moves with second-order implications
- Sharp pieces of discourse from people the user already tracks (populate that list in MEMORY.md as it grows)

Tie-breaker when picking 2–3 items:
1. Lay-stakes legibility — can a non-specialist friend be told why it matters in one sentence?
2. Recency — sharper if it's the same day.
3. Voice fit — does it match the user's accepted-drafts patterns in MEMORY.md (if any)?
