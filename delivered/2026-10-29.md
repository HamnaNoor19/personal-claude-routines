Almost every AI model you use was trained and served on chips from one supplier or one company's own cloud, and that is starting to loosen. On Oct 27, Oracle and Google said Google's TPUs will be rentable on Oracle's cloud.

TPUs are Google's custom AI chips, an alternative to Nvidia's GPUs. Until now, to use them you generally had to be a Google Cloud customer. Oracle says it will be the first major cloud outside Google to offer them, with availability in 2027 ramping over several years. [TODO: verify "first hyperscaler" wording]

Why this matters beyond chip nerds: a company that wants to run a large model has had a short menu, and the menu decides prices and who can build what. Oracle also reportedly plans to add OpenAI's own chip, called Jalapeño, and Cerebras hardware, the latter starting in the first half of 2027.
